There is a system of sorting information called „LATCH“. It’s an organizing principle developed by Richard Saul Wurman in his 1989 book Information Anxiety. It states that almost any form of unstructured information or physical object can be organized in exactly five basic ways. This means you can sort your after Location, Alphabet, Time, Category and Hierachy. We could be using this technique for taking the minutes even. —
